FAQ

Everything you need to know before placing your order.

Where do you ship?

We ship pan India. For international shipments, please email us at mnrstudio01@gmail.com.

When will my order be delivered?

Our standard delivery timeline is 3–5 working days. During periods of high order volume or unforeseen circumstances, dispatch may occasionally take slightly longer. If this happens, our team will inform you via email or WhatsApp with an update. Saturdays, Sundays, and public holidays are not considered working days, and transit delays can occasionally occur.

Can I cancel my order?

We do not accept cancellations once the order is confirmed. We kindly request customers to place orders only if they are certain about the purchase.

Do you offer Cash on Delivery (COD)?

No, we do not accept cash on delivery.

Do you offer exchanges?

Yes, we offer size exchanges if the size does not fit. Exchange requests must be raised within 48 hours of delivery. The exact packaging is required so the item reaches safely. Exchanges are applicable only for size changes of the same product, subject to availability. Items must be unused, unwashed, and in original condition with tags intact. Products showing signs of wear, stains, perfume, makeup marks, washing, or alteration are not eligible for exchange.

Is there a fee for exchanges?

Yes. A ₹150 exchange fee is applicable to cover re-shipping and logistics charges.

Do you accept returns or refunds?

We currently do not offer returns or refunds. However, we do support size exchanges as per our exchange policy.

What should I do if I receive a damaged item?

All items undergo strict quality checks before dispatch. In the rare case that you receive a damaged or defective item, please contact us within 24 hours of delivery and share clear photos plus an unboxing video showing the issue. Requests raised without an unboxing video may not be eligible for replacement.

How can I get featured on your page?

We love seeing how you style our pieces. To get featured, email your photos to mnrstudio01@gmail.com or tag us on Instagram at @mnr__studio.

Who is responsible for additional shipping charges if an order is returned due to incorrect details provided by the customer?

If an order is returned to us due to incorrect details provided by the customer, the customer will be responsible for the additional shipping charges.

How do I choose the right size?

If you are unsure about sizing, we recommend DMing us before placing your order. Our team will happily help you choose the correct size.

How can I contact you?

You can reach us through Instagram DM at @mnr__studio, email at mnrstudio01@gmail.com, or WhatsApp at 8928543726. Our team will be happy to assist you.

Still have questions? DM us on Instagram at @mnr__studio or email mnrstudio01@gmail.com.

Refund & Return Policy

Please read our policy carefully before placing your order.

Orders cannot be cancelled once placed.

By placing an order with M&R, you acknowledge that you have read and agreed to all policies mentioned below.

Returns & Exchanges

We understand that sometimes the size may not work out, and we are happy to assist with size exchanges.

  • Exchange requests must be raised within 48 hours of delivery.
  • Exchanges are applicable only for size changes of the same product, subject to availability.
  • Items must be unused, unwashed, and returned in original condition with tags intact.

To be eligible for an exchange, please email us at mnrstudio01@gmail.com within 48 hours of receiving the product. Kindly attach clear pictures of the issue.

For approved exchanges, customers are required to pay a ₹150 exchange fee to cover re-shipping and logistics charges.

If the exchanged parcel needs to be shipped again due to an incorrect address, unavailability, or refusal, additional shipping charges may apply.

However, if you receive the wrong size from our side, or an item is damaged or defective from our side, you can request a replacement or refund.

Damaged or Defective Items

All products go through quality checks before dispatch. If you receive a damaged or defective item:

  • Please contact us within 24 hours of delivery.
  • Share clear photos and an unboxing video showing the issue.
  • Requests raised without an unboxing video may not be eligible for replacement, as we are unable to verify claims afterward.

Refunds

Refunds are only issued if we are unable to fulfill your order due to unforeseen circumstances.

If your replacement request is approved and we cannot provide a suitable exchange, we may offer a refund.

Refunds will be processed within 7–10 business days through the original payment method.

Cancellation Policy

Once an order is placed, it cannot be canceled, modified, or refunded. Please review your order carefully before confirming your purchase.

Lost or Wrong Orders

If your parcel is marked “Delivered” but not received, contact us within 24 hours so we can escalate the issue with our shipping partner.

If you receive a wrong or defective item, send us clear pictures within 24 hours of delivery. If approved, we will offer a replacement, provided all tags are intact.

Size & Fit Assistance

If you are unsure about sizing, we strongly recommend DMing us before placing your order. Our team will gladly help you choose the right size.
